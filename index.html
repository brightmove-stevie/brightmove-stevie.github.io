BrightMove: evidence for the 2027 Stevie Awards entries

BrightMove is a cloud-based applicant tracking system (ATS) and talent acquisition platform for staffing agencies, HR teams, PEOs and RPO providers. As a standalone ATS, its customers' success depends on integrations and on the support behind them. BrightMove earned Indeed Platinum ATS Partner status in Q4 2025.

BrightMove Customer Feedback Loop: how customers give feedback, how every negative score is reviewed weekly, and how the loop reaches the product.

Entry 2: Human-Centered AI Innovation of the Year

BrightMove Wiz and Support Team: an AI agent that extends first-line support to every hour, with people taking over escalations and auditing the AI weekly.
