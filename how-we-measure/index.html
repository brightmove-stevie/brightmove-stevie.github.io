How the figures are measured

Definitions, filters and date ranges for every figure on this site.

Date range

All headline figures cover October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026. Dashboard history begins October 2025. The feedback loop began operating in July 2025.

CX score (headline measure)

An AI-inferred score from 1 to 5 assigned to closed customer conversations. It requires no action from the customer. Scores of 4–5 count as positive.

CSAT (cross-check)

A voluntary 1–5 star rating offered at the end of a chat. Ratings of 4–5 count as positive. [PENDING: confirm whether Wiz-handled conversations are included]
