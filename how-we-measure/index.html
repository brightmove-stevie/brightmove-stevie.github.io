How the figures are measured

Definitions, filters and date ranges for every figure on this site.

Source and date range

All figures come from Intercom Reports (Overview) and cover Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026 (New York time). Dashboard history begins October 2025. The feedback loop began operating in July 2025. All screenshots were captured on Oct 27, 2026.

CX score (headline measure)

An AI-inferred score from 1 to 5 assigned to closed customer conversations. It requires no action from the customer. Scores of 4–5 count as positive. CX percentage = conversations scored 4–5 ÷ conversations with a CX score.

When a conversation gets a CX score

CX scoring is a feature of Intercom's AI. A conversation is scored only when all of these are true:

  • It is closed.
  • It happened over chat or email, not phone.
  • The customer started it.
  • It has at least 2 customer replies and 2 agent or chatbot replies.
  • It has enough clear information for the model to evaluate.
  • It isn't spam.

The model then weighs signals such as answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback to assign a CX rating from 1 to 5. Only 3,272 of the 4,840 counted conversations met these conditions and have a CX score.

The four CX scores

Intercom reports four CX scores. Definitions are adapted from Intercom's report tiles:

  • Overall CX: how well support is working for customers. AI rates each conversation on answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback.
  • Wiz-only CX: how well Wiz is working for customers. AI rates each conversation handled only by Wiz on answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback.
  • Wiz + teammate CX: how well Wiz and the team work together. AI rates each conversation handled by both on answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback.
  • Teammate-only CX: how well the support team is working for customers. AI rates each conversation handled only by teammates on answer quality, customer effort, emotion, and product or policy feedback.

Naming note: Wiz is BrightMove's AI agent. In Intercom it appears as "Fin AI Agent", so Intercom's tiles read "Fin AI Agent CX Score" (Wiz-only) and "Fin AI Agent and Teammate CX Score" (Wiz + teammate).

CSAT (cross-check)

A voluntary 1–5 star rating offered at the end of the same chat. Ratings of 4–5 count as positive. The overall CSAT figure includes ratings on Wiz-handled conversations; Wiz CSAT and teammate CSAT are also shown separately.

Why CX is the headline

CX covers far more conversations than CSAT, because it needs no customer action. CSAT is the cross-check.

The filter used for every figure

Every figure on this site is based on 4,840 Intercom conversations from Oct 1, 2025 to Sep 30, 2026 (New York time), after removing conversations that are not customer support.

How the 4,840 counted conversations are reached
Conversations
All conversations in the period5,524
Removed: tagged SPAM−345
Removed: tagged Testing−44
Removed: tagged account maintenance (Intercom tag "Non-LVE Related CX")−295
Counted in every figure4,840

299 conversations carry the account maintenance tag; 4 of them are also tagged SPAM or Testing and are counted once, under SPAM or Testing.

  • Why SPAM and Testing are removed: they are not real customer conversations.
  • Why account maintenance is removed: these requests (such as user adds and setting changes) are routine admin tasks, not customer-experience conversations. They are reviewed separately each week to decide which tasks to make self-service.
  • What the tag name means: LVE stands for LightHub Veracity Enhancement, a conversation that shows something in the knowledge base or the Wiz AI agent needs refining. "Non-LVE Related CX" marks conversations that need no such refinement, such as account maintenance requests. Manual account-maintenance tagging began in late October 2025, so a small number of October 2025 conversations may be included.

About the screenshots

  • All screenshots were captured on Oct 27, 2026 from Intercom Reports, using the date range and filter above.
  • The small green/red arrows next to each dashboard tile are Intercom's previous-period comparison. Intercom offers no toggle to hide them, and dashboard history begins October 2025, so they are not a meaningful comparison and are not cited on this site.
  • The sample-size screenshots are cropped to the filter bar and item count; no conversation rows are shown. Intercom cuts off the filter label at the right edge, so the full filter is written out in the section above: Excludes conversations tagged Conversation: SPAM, Conversation: Testing, or Conversation: Non-LVE Related CX (account maintenance).
  • On the Wiz performance screenshot (Entry 2), the Resolution rate tile has been masked with a grey box because the resolution definition is pending confirmation; the chart is unaltered.

Why figures move

Intercom reporting is dynamic: conversations reopen and ratings arrive late. The screenshots captured on Oct 27, 2026 are the source of truth for every figure on this site.
