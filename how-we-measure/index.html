How the figures are measured

Definitions, filters and date ranges for every figure on this site.

Source and date range

All figures come from Intercom Reports (Overview) and cover Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026 (New York time). Dashboard history begins October 2025. The feedback loop began operating in July 2025. Captured Oct 27, 2026.

CX score (headline measure)

An AI-inferred score from 1 to 5 assigned to closed customer conversations. It requires no action from the customer. Scores of 4–5 count as positive. CX percentage = conversations scored 4–5 ÷ conversations with a CX score.

CSAT (cross-check)

A voluntary 1–5 star rating offered at the end of the same chat. Ratings of 4–5 count as positive. The overall CSAT figure includes ratings on Wiz-handled conversations; Wiz CSAT and teammate CSAT are also shown separately.

Why CX is the headline

CX covers far more conversations than CSAT, because it needs no customer action. CSAT is the cross-check. Sample sizes: CX [PENDING: N conversations with a CX score]; CSAT [PENDING: N ratings].

Why figures move

Intercom reporting is dynamic: conversations reopen and ratings arrive late. The screenshots captured on Oct 27, 2026 are the source of truth for every figure on this site.
