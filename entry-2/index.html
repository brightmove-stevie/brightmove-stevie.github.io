BrightMove Wiz and Support Team. Category 227. AI that extends human support rather than replacing it.

1. The model

Shows: Wiz handles routine questions at any hour; people take over what it cannot resolve.

  • Tier 1, Wiz (AI agent built on Intercom Fin): instant answers to common questions, 24/7, 365 days a year.
  • Tier 2, BrightMove's support team: escalated and relationship-sensitive issues; target response under 2 hours.
  • Tier 3, engineering: bugs and integration failures; target under 24 hours.

Escalation rule: if Wiz cannot resolve a conversation in two attempts, a human agent takes over with the full transcript and owns the outcome. Customers always have a path to a person by chat, phone or email.
